Triggers

Each trigger fires independently. When several are active at once, the screen pulse shows the most urgent one's color; all of them appear in the menu-bar list. Toggle one off to silence it without removing it.

Major Incident

Fires instance-wide when any open Jira ticket is flagged as a Major Incident. Works with Jira Software or Jira Service Management.

    SLA

    Fires for watched users when a Jira Service Management SLA is about to breach or already has.

      Approval

      Fires when you have pending approvals (service requests, changes) waiting in Jira Service Management.

        Teams

        Fires when a watched user sends you a Microsoft Teams message that goes unread past your threshold.

          Email

          Fires when a watched user sends you a Microsoft Outlook email that stays unread past your threshold. Pick people on the trigger card below.
