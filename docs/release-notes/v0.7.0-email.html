What's new in Nowtify
This update makes long-running incidents easier to live with and gives you more room to see your alerts.
 
Acknowledge long-running alerts
When a Major Incident or SLA alert has been open for a while, click the Nowtify icon and click Acknowledge on its row. It stops pulsing on your screen edge and the Nowtify icon, so other alerts show through instead of being hidden behind it. The alert stays in the list, dimmed, until the ticket is resolved. Changed your mind? Click Acknowledged to undo. It only affects your own Nowtify.
Bigger, resizable alert list
The alert list now opens larger, so more alerts fit without scrolling. Drag any edge to make it the size you want; Nowtify remembers it next time.
Pulse hidden from screen shares
When you share your screen, the people watching no longer see the Nowtify pulse around its edge. You still do. On Windows this needs Windows 10 (version 2004) or later.
Fixed: approvals with no title
Some pending approvals showed only the ticket number and requester. They now show the ticket's title.
How to get it
Nowtify downloads the update for you. When it's ready, you'll see Update ready at the top of the alert list (also Install update when you right-click the Nowtify icon). Click it and Nowtify restarts on the new version. If IT installed Nowtify on your Windows PC, IT rolls the update out for you.
