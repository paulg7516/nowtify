What's new in Nowtify
This update includes a fix for an update pop-up that kept coming back on some Macs, and makes Nowtify updates simpler for your organization to manage for you.
 
Fixed: update pop-up that kept coming back on Mac
After the last release, some Macs kept showing "Version 0.7.0 is ready to install". Clicking Restart now couldn't replace the app, so Nowtify reopened on the old version and asked again. Nowtify now recognizes when your IT administrator manages it and no longer shows that pop-up.
Updates on company-managed and other devices
On a company-managed device, your organization keeps Nowtify up to date for you. On a non-company-managed device, you update Nowtify yourself: when a new version is ready, Nowtify notifies you and you can install it.
Did you know? When you share your screen, the people watching don't see the Nowtify pulse around your screen edge. You still do, so you won't miss an alert while presenting.
