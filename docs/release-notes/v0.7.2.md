## v0.7.2

- **Fixed: the "Version 0.7.0 is ready to install" message kept coming back on Macs.** On Macs where Nowtify was installed by IT, clicking **Restart now** couldn't replace the app, so it reopened on the old version and asked again. Nowtify now recognizes copies installed by IT and leaves updates to IT. On Macs where you installed Nowtify yourself, updates now install more safely: if anything goes wrong, your current version stays in place and you won't be asked again for that version.
- **Simpler Updates screen when IT manages Nowtify.** If your organization installed Nowtify for you, Settings > Updates now shows your version and "Kept up to date by your organization. If a new version exists, your IT administrator will install it." There's no update button to click and no update messages in the menu or alerts window.
