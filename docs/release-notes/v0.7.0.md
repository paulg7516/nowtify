## v0.7.0

- **Acknowledge long-running alerts.** When a Major Incident or SLA alert has been open for a while, click the Nowtify icon (menu bar on Mac, system tray on Windows) and click **Acknowledge** on its row. It stops pulsing on your screen edge and the Nowtify icon, so any other alert shows through instead of being hidden behind it. The alert stays in the list, dimmed, until the ticket is resolved. Changed your mind? Click **Acknowledged** to undo. Acknowledging is per alert and only affects your own Nowtify.

- **Bigger, resizable alert list.** The Nowtify window now opens larger, so more alerts fit without scrolling. Drag any edge to make it the size you want; Nowtify remembers it next time.

- **Pulse hidden from screen shares.** When you share your screen, the people watching no longer see the Nowtify pulse around its edge. You still do. On Windows this needs Windows 10 (version 2004) or later.

- **Fixed: approvals with no title.** Some pending approvals showed only the ticket number and requester. They now show the ticket's title.

- **IT-installed copies on Windows.** If IT installed Nowtify on your PC, Nowtify now shows when a newer version is available, grayed out with a note that it's kept up to date by your IT administrator, instead of offering to install it itself. IT rolls the update out.
