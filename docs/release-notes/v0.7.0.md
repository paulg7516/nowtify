## v0.7.0

- **Acknowledge long-running alerts.** When a Major Incident or SLA alert has been open for a while, open Nowtify from the menu bar and click **Acknowledge** on its row. It stops pulsing on your screen edge and menu-bar icon, so any other alert shows through instead of being hidden behind it. The alert stays in the list, dimmed, until the ticket is resolved. Changed your mind? Click **Acknowledged** to undo. Acknowledging is per alert and only affects your own Nowtify.

- **Bigger, resizable alert list.** The Nowtify window now opens larger, so more alerts fit without scrolling. Drag any edge to make it the size you want; Nowtify remembers it next time.
